HOW IT WORKS

Four hops from overwhelmed to ahh.

STEP 1

Hop on a call or we hop on over to you!

Tell me about the space, the stuff, and what's driving you up the wall. Free and no pressure.

STEP 2

The big sort

We go through it all together, sorting into keep, sell and donate piles. You decide; I keep us moving.

STEP 3

The edit

Everything you keep gets a home: the right containers, clear labels, and a system you can actually keep up.

STEP 4

The finishing touches

Sold pieces shipped, donations dropped off, and a thrifted find or two to make the room feel finished.
